# Riyadh Residential Land Acquisition Study

An investment decision study comparing two residential land acquisition opportunities in Riyadh, built the way a Research & Investment team inside a real estate developer would assess them.

## Decision Question

Which of the two opportunities should we acquire, if either, at what maximum bid for each, and with what product, unit mix and phasing, such that the project IRR exceeds the target hurdle in the base case and remains above the minimum threshold in the downside case?

## The Two Opportunities

| | Al-Arid | Al-Khalidiya |
|---|---|---|
| Market | North Riyadh | Central Riyadh |
| Land area | 10,034.81 m² | 18,591.75 m² |
| Asking price | 6,500 SAR/m² | 2,700 SAR/m² |
| Total asking price | 65,226,265 SAR | 50,197,725 SAR |

## Decision Options

1. Acquire Al-Arid only
2. Acquire Al-Khalidiya only
3. Acquire both, phased
4. Negotiate one at a maximum bid
5. Reject both

## Methodology

Affordability → Supply-Demand Gap → Hedonic Pricing → Unit Mix Optimization (Linear Programming) → Development Feasibility (DCF) → Scenarios & Sensitivity → Decision
